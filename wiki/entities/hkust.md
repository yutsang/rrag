## 概述

香港科技大學（HKUST）是一所位於香港清水灣的研究型大學，以工程、商學及科技領域見長。本知識庫收錄了其工程管理碩士（EEMT）、商學碩士（SBMT）及大數據科技碩士（MSBD）等課程的教材摘要。

## Overview

The **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)** is a research-intensive university located in Clear Water Bay, Hong Kong. Founded in 1991, HKUST has rapidly established itself as one of Asia's leading universities, particularly in engineering, business, and technology disciplines.

The courses documented in this wiki span multiple graduate programs including the Master of Science in Engineering Enterprise Management (EEMT), Master of Science in Business (SBMT), and Master of Science in Big Data Technology (MSBD).

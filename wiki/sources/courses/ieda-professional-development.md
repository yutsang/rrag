## 概述

IEDA 1010/1020 是香港科技大學工業工程及決策分析學系（IEDA）的入門必修課，以通過／不通過方式評核，分別於秋季（1010）及春季（1020）開設。課程面向大一本科生，不設成績，旨在透過業界嘉賓演講、校友分享及師生交流，幫助學生建立對工業工程、決策分析與物流管理三大學科方向的整體認識。每學期學生須就一位嘉賓演講撰寫不少於 500 字的個人報告，涵蓋引言、主體及對職業方向的反思。課程由 Prof. Guillermo Gallego 主持，強調表達溝通能力與定量工具的早期接觸，為後續 IEDA2010（工業工程概覽）及所有專業課程奠定學術與職業發展基礎。

## Course Overview

**IEDA 1010/1020: Academic and Professional Development I/II** are compulsory pass/fail orientation courses for IEDA undergraduates at [[wiki/entities/hkust|HKUST]]. Offered across two semesters (Fall = 1010, Spring = 1020), they expose students to the full landscape of Industrial Engineering, Decision Analytics, and Logistics Management through structured seminars and reflective assignments.

- **Instructor**: Prof. Guillermo Gallego (Room 5545)
- **Format**: Weekly seminars via Zoom (Fall 2020); 1 hour per week
- **Assessment**: Pass/Fail only — no grades assigned

## Course Structure

The course runs five parallel activity tracks:

| Activity | Description |
|---|---|
| Presentations by Speakers | Industry practitioners present their sector and opportunities for IEs |
| Alumni Talks | IEDA graduates share school experiences and career paths |
| Advisory Meetings | Students meet advisors individually or in groups |
| Student Faculty Meetings | Students give feedback on their current IEDA courses |
| Individual Report | 500+ word reflective essay on a guest speaker from the semester |

## Learning Outcomes

Students completing the course are expected to:
1. Describe key concepts and principles of industrial engineering, decision analytics, and logistics management.
2. Formulate and solve simple IE, decision analytics, and logistics management problems.
3. Communicate effectively, both orally and in writing.
4. Apply basic quantitative methods and appropriate software tools to IE/analytics problems.

## Significance for IEDA Students

These courses serve as the primary socialization mechanism for IEDA undergraduates, providing:
- **Industry exposure**: Direct contact with practicing engineers and managers in Hong Kong.
- **Peer network**: Connections with alumni who have navigated the same program.
- **Career orientation**: Early clarity on what IE/analytics roles look like in practice.
- **Reflective practice**: The individual report develops written communication around technical careers.

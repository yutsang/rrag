## 概述

FINA1203 個人財務規劃為非金融專業的一年級學生提供個人理財的基礎知識，無特定先修要求，是工商管理學院最廣泛受眾的通識財務課程之一。課程以香港金融環境為背景，涵蓋個人財務報表（淨資產與現金流量表）、預算管理（50/30/20 法則）、貨幣時間價值的實際應用（儲蓄目標的現值計算）、香港強制性公積金（MPF）制度、銀行存款與信用卡產品比較，以及人壽保險（定期對比終身）和退休規劃的概念框架。學生完成課程後能夠建立個人資產負債表、計算貸款攤還計劃、評估投資產品的風險回報特性，以及理解分散投資的基本原理。雖然本課程數學深度較淺，但其建立的財務概念框架（機會成本、流動性與回報的取捨、預期值在保險決策中的應用）是理解企業財務與投資學高年級課程的直覺基礎，對工程及理科學生培養財務素養尤為重要。

## Course Overview

Entry-level finance course for students outside the FINA major. Practical orientation — less math, more decision-making frameworks for personal wealth management.

## Key Topics

### Financial Planning Foundations
- Personal financial statements: net worth (assets − liabilities), cash flow statement
- Budgeting: 50/30/20 rule; tracking income and expenses
- Time value of money basics: compound interest, present value of savings goals — see [[time-value-of-money]]
- Emergency fund: 3-6 months expenses; liquidity vs. return trade-off

### Banking and Credit
- Bank account types: savings, checking, time deposits; interest rate comparisons
- Credit cards: APR, minimum payments, debt spiral mathematics
- Loans: mortgages, auto loans, student loans; amortization schedules
- Credit scores: factors (payment history, utilization, length of credit history)

### Investments
- Asset classes: cash, bonds, stocks, real estate, alternatives
- Risk-return trade-off; diversification benefits — see [[portfolio-theory]]
- Mutual funds vs. ETFs: fees (expense ratio, MER), passive vs. active management
- MPF (Mandatory Provident Fund) in Hong Kong: contribution rules, fund choices
- Tax considerations in Hong Kong: salaries tax, profits tax

### Insurance
- Types: life, health, disability, property/casualty
- Term vs. whole life insurance; calculating coverage needs
- Expected value framework for insurance decisions

### Retirement Planning
- Goal-setting: retirement income needs, replacement rate (70-80% of pre-retirement income)
- Accumulation vs. decumulation phases
- ORSO (Occupational Retirement Schemes) in HK context
- Annuities: fixed vs. variable; payout options

### Estate Planning (intro)
- Wills; intestacy rules in Hong Kong
- Power of attorney; beneficiary designations

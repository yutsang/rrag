## 概述

ACCT2200 管理會計為工商管理學院二年級學生教授如何運用成本信息支持企業內部決策，以 Garrison、Noreen 及 Brewer《管理會計》（Managerial Accounting，McGraw-Hill 標準教材）或 Horngren《成本會計》為參考，先修要求為 ACCT2010 會計學原理，著重與財務會計（對外報告）截然不同的對內決策支援功能。課程系統覆蓋成本行為分析（固定成本、變動成本、混合成本的高低點法與迴歸分解）、訂單成本制（以預定分攤率分配製造費用）與流程成本制（當量產出計算）、作業成本制（ABC 的成本動因識別與交叉補貼分析）、CVP 分析（盈虧平衡點、安全邊際、營業槓桿倍數）、標準成本制的差異分析（材料價格與數量差異、人工效率差異、製造費用差異）及彈性預算與責任中心（投資報酬率、剩餘收益、EVA）的績效評估。決策相關章節涵蓋自製或外購分析、剔除產品線決策、特殊訂單接受準則及稀缺資源下的邊際貢獻最大化（線性規劃概念）。學生完成課程後能夠建立完整的標準成本卡、分析預算差異的根本原因，以及為製造業與服務業的短期定價與長期資本預算決策提供準確的成本資訊支撐。

## Course Overview

Course is structured in 11 chapters (Chapter 1-11), each with lecture slides and solutions provided. Covers cost accounting for internal decision-making, as distinct from financial accounting for external reporting.

## Key Topics

### Cost Concepts and Classification
- **Product costs** vs. **period costs**: manufacturing vs. non-manufacturing
- Direct materials, direct labor, manufacturing overhead; prime cost vs. conversion cost
- **Fixed vs. variable costs**: behavior with respect to volume
- **Mixed costs**: high-low method; regression to separate fixed/variable components
- Relevant costs for decisions: differential, opportunity, sunk (irrelevant)
- See [[managerial-accounting]]

### Job-Order and Process Costing
- **Job-order costing**: custom production; job cost sheet; overhead allocation using predetermined rate
- **Process costing**: continuous production (chemicals, food); equivalent units; weighted average vs. FIFO methods
- Spoilage: normal vs. abnormal; treatment in cost systems

### Activity-Based Costing (ABC)
- Limitation of volume-based overhead rates: cost distortion for low-volume/complex products
- ABC procedure: identify activities → assign costs to activity pools → select cost drivers → assign to products
- ABC vs. traditional: exposes cross-subsidization; better decision support
- See [[managerial-accounting]]

### CVP Analysis
- Contribution margin: $CM = Revenue - Variable Costs$; CM ratio
- Break-even point: $Q_{BE} = \frac{Fixed Costs}{CM per unit}$
- Margin of safety; operating leverage: $OL = \frac{CM}{Net Income}$
- Multi-product CVP: weighted average CM; sales mix assumptions
- See [[cvp-analysis]]

### Standard Costing and Variance Analysis
- Standard costs: ideal vs. practical standards; standard cost card
- **Direct materials variances**: price variance, quantity variance
- **Direct labor variances**: rate variance, efficiency variance
- **Overhead variances**: spending variance, efficiency variance, volume variance (for fixed OH)
- Variance investigation: statistical control chart approach
- See [[managerial-accounting]]

### Flexible Budgeting and Performance Evaluation
- Static vs. flexible budget: volume differences eliminate apples-to-oranges comparisons
- Responsibility centers: cost, revenue, profit, investment centers
- ROI, residual income, EVA for investment center evaluation

### Relevant Costs for Decisions
- Make-or-buy: incremental costs only; qualitative factors (quality, reliability)
- Drop a product/segment: avoidable vs. unavoidable fixed costs
- Special order: accept if price > variable costs (short run, with capacity)
- Constrained optimization: maximize CM per unit of scarce resource — see [[linear-programming]]

### Capital Budgeting
- NPV, IRR, payback; tax effects; depreciation tax shield — see [[npv]], [[irr]]
- Post-audit; behavioral issues in capital budgeting

## 概述

MGMT2110 組織行為學為工商管理二年級學生深入研究個人、群體及組織三個層面的行為規律，以 Robbins & Judge《組織行為學》（Organizational Behavior，Pearson 全球標準教材）為核心教材，先修要求為 MGMT1110 管理學概論，是 MGMT1110 管理理論框架的行為科學深化。課程系統整合心理學、社會心理學與組織理論的實證研究，涵蓋大五人格（OCEAN）與工作績效的預測關係、歸因理論（基本歸因謬誤、自利偏誤）、工作滿意度的認知失調動態、Locke 目標設定理論與 Bandura 自我效能理論的激勵應用、Tuckman 群體發展階段模型、社會懈怠的對抗機制、Fisher & Ury 原則性談判（BATNA）與 Thomas-Kilmann 衝突管理模式，以及 Lewin 三步驟與 Kotter 八步驟變革管理框架。學生完成課程後能夠診斷組織中的群體思維症狀並設計預防機制、設計激勵薪酬結構（基於期望理論的工具性連結）、評估組織文化對策略實施的促進或阻礙作用，以及運用整合性談判框架擴大談判空間。行為科學視角對運籌學與工業工程研究亦具補充價值，特別是在人機系統設計、激勵相容機制設計及組織決策偏差的緩解策略方面。

## Course Overview

**Textbook**: Robbins & Judge, *Organizational Behavior* (standard edition)

OB is the behavioral science foundation for managing people in organizations. Content extends beyond MGMT1110's management overview to provide deeper treatment of individual and group behavior.

## Key Topics

### Individual Behavior

**Personality and Values**:
- Big Five personality traits (OCEAN): Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism
- Myers-Briggs (MBTI): limitations for organizational prediction
- Person-job, person-organization fit; implications for selection and satisfaction
- Work values: Rokeach Value Survey; terminal vs. instrumental values

**Perception and Attribution**:
- Perceptual process: selective attention; organization; interpretation
- Attribution theory: internal vs. external causes; fundamental attribution error; self-serving bias
- Common perceptual errors: halo effect, contrast effects, stereotyping, projection
- Self-fulfilling prophecy (Pygmalion effect)

**Attitudes and Job Satisfaction**:
- Cognitive dissonance theory (Festinger): attitude change under inconsistency
- Job satisfaction determinants: work itself, supervision, pay, promotion, co-workers
- Consequences: performance, OCB (organizational citizenship behavior), turnover, absenteeism
- Organizational commitment: affective, continuance, normative

**Motivation (Advanced)**:
- Goal-setting theory (Locke): specific and challenging goals; feedback; moderators
- Self-efficacy; social cognitive theory (Bandura)
- Reinforcement theory: schedules of reinforcement; behavioral modification
- Intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation; crowding out effect
- See [[organizational-behavior]]

### Group Behavior

**Group Dynamics**:
- Group formation: Tuckman's stages (forming, storming, norming, performing, adjourning)
- Social loafing: diffusion of responsibility; countermeasures
- Groupthink: symptoms; prevention (devil's advocate, dialectical inquiry)
- Group cohesiveness: relationship to performance (moderating variables)

**Communication**:
- Communication process: sender → encoding → channel → decoding → receiver; noise
- Formal vs. informal channels; grapevine; electronic communication pros/cons
- Active listening; non-verbal communication; cross-cultural communication barriers

**Teams**:
- Work groups vs. work teams; types: problem-solving, self-managed, cross-functional, virtual
- Team effectiveness model: context, composition, work design, process
- Trust in teams: calculus-based, knowledge-based, identification-based

**Power and Politics**:
- Bases of power (French & Raven): legitimate, reward, coercive, expert, referent
- Influence tactics: rational persuasion, inspirational appeals, coalition building
- Organizational politics: conditions conducive to political behavior; impression management

**Conflict and Negotiation**:
- Conflict types: task, relationship, process
- Conflict management styles: competing, collaborating, compromising, avoiding, accommodating (Thomas-Kilmann)
- Negotiation: distributive (zero-sum) vs. integrative; BATNA; principled negotiation (Fisher & Ury)
- See [[game-theory]]

### Organizational Level

**Organizational Structure**:
- Mechanistic vs. organic; determinants: strategy, size, technology, environment
- Matrix structures; team-based organizations; virtual organizations

**Organizational Culture**:
- Socialization: prearrival, encounter, metamorphosis stages
- Culture change: leadership signals; symbolic actions; hiring/promotion decisions

**Change Management**:
- Lewin's 3-step model: unfreeze → change → refreeze
- Kotter's 8-step model; change resistance: individual and organizational sources
- OD (Organizational Development) interventions: survey feedback, process consultation, team building

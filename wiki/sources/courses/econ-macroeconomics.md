## 概述

ECON2123 宏觀經濟學理論為經濟系二年級學生提供短期波動、中期均衡與長期增長的統一分析框架，以 Blanchard《宏觀經濟學》（Macroeconomics，Pearson 標準教材）或 Mankiw《宏觀經濟學》（中級版）為主要教材，先修要求為 ECON2103 及大學一年級微積分，以便處理 IS-LM 的代數求解與 Solow 模型的微分分析。課程以 IS-LM-PC 框架為核心，整合了商品市場均衡（IS 曲線）、金融市場均衡（LM 曲線或中央銀行利率規則）及菲利普斯曲線（通脹-失業動態），並將其嵌入 AD-AS 模型分析需求與供給衝擊的動態調整。長期增長模塊深入 Solow 資本積累模型（黃金率儲蓄率、條件收斂）及 Romer 內生增長理論（知識溢出、研發投資）。開放經濟章節介紹 Mundell-Fleming 模型，分析固定與浮動匯率制度下財政與貨幣政策的有效性，並以香港聯繫匯率制度（貨幣局制度）為現實案例。學生完成課程後能夠運用 Taylor 規則評估貨幣政策立場、分析財政乘數效應、理解通脹預期與去通脹代價（犧牲率），以及評估開放小型經濟體的外部衝擊傳導機制，為金融市場分析、政策研究及宏觀風險管理提供理論基礎。

## Course Overview

**Textbook**: Olivier Blanchard, *Macroeconomics* (or Mankiw's intermediate macro text)

Covers macroeconomic theory with mathematical rigor — IS-LM-PC (Phillips curve) framework for short/medium run; Solow and Romer for long run.

## Key Topics

### National Accounts and Measurement
- GDP: expenditure, income, and value-added methods; chain-weighting
- CPI vs. GDP deflator; inflation measurement biases (substitution, quality)
- Unemployment: CPS methodology; labor force participation; Okun's law: $\Delta U \approx -0.5 \Delta Y$

### Goods Market and IS Curve
- Goods market equilibrium: $Y = C + I + G + NX$
- Consumption function: $C = c_0 + c_1(Y-T)$; MPC; multiplier
- Investment function: depends on output and interest rate $r$
- IS curve: all $(Y, r)$ pairs where goods market clears; slopes downward
- Fiscal policy: multiplier effects; balanced-budget multiplier = 1

### Financial Markets and LM Curve
- Money demand: $M^d/P = L(Y, i)$; transactions motive
- LM curve (traditional): money market equilibrium
- Modern approach: central bank sets $i$ (interest rate rule replaces LM)
- Liquidity trap: zero lower bound; quantitative easing

### IS-LM-PC Framework
- Short run: prices fixed; output demand-determined
- Medium run: Phillips curve $\pi - \pi^e = (m + z) - \alpha u$; unemployment gap drives inflation
- Long-run natural rate of unemployment ($u_n$); NAIRU
- Monetary policy rule: Taylor rule $i = i^* + a(\pi - \pi^*) + b(Y - Y^n)$

### Aggregate Supply and Demand
- AD curve: downward sloping in $(Y, P)$; monetary/fiscal policy shifts it
- AS curve: upward sloping in short run; vertical (classical) in long run
- Supply shocks: oil prices, wage push; stagflation
- Dynamic adjustment: self-correcting mechanism vs. policy intervention

### Open Economy Macroeconomics
- Balance of payments: current account (CA), capital account (KA), financial account
- Exchange rate: nominal vs. real; PPP; interest rate parity
- **Mundell-Fleming model** (open economy IS-LM):
  - Fixed exchange rate: fiscal policy effective, monetary policy ineffective
  - Floating exchange rate: monetary policy effective, fiscal policy crowded out via exchange rate
- HK currency board: zero monetary independence; full capital mobility

### Long-Run Growth
- **Solow model**: $Y = K^\alpha (AL)^{1-\alpha}$; steady state $k^* = \left(\frac{s}{n+g+\delta}\right)^{1/(1-\alpha)}$
- Golden rule savings rate; capital overaccumulation
- Convergence: conditional vs. unconditional; catch-up growth in East Asia
- **Endogenous growth**: Romer AK model; knowledge spillovers; R&D-based growth

### Inflation and Unemployment
- Short-run Phillips curve trade-off
- Long-run vertical Phillips curve (Friedman-Phelps natural rate hypothesis)
- Disinflation: sacrifice ratio; credibility and expectations
- Hysteresis: temporary shocks with permanent unemployment effects

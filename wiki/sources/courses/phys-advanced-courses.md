## 概述

PHYS1112、PHYS1114 及 PHYS1312 為香港科技大學理工科一年級學生提供大學程度物理的嚴格訓練，以 Halliday、Resnick 及 Krane《物理學》（Physics，Wiley 標準教材）或 Walker 版本為標準教材，先修要求為高中物理與微積分（或同期修讀 MATH1012），面向工程、物理及數學專業學生。PHYS1112 以向量處理力學（慣性矩張量、剛體滾動）、阻尼與受迫振動（共振現象）及嚴格熱力學（熵的 Clausius 不等式、統計詮釋）；PHYS1114 深入電磁場論（Gauss 定律與 Ampere 定律的微分形式 $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \rho/\varepsilon_0$、Maxwell 方程組與電磁波推導、Poynting 向量能量通量）及近代物理（狹義相對論的時間膨脹與質能等效、量子力學的光電效應、de Broglie 波長、Heisenberg 測不準原理、Bohr 氫原子模型）；榮譽版 PHYS1312 在相同框架下加入向量微積分的微分形式（散度、旋度、Stokes 定理）及 Lagrangian 力學（最小作用量原理）。學生完成課程後具備以向量微積分語言分析力學與電磁系統、理解量子力學測量不確定性的物理直覺，以及對材料半導體性質（能帶理論初步）的基本認識，為電機工程、材料科學、計算物理及量子計算的進一步學習奠定堅實的數學物理基礎。

## PHYS1112: General Physics I

### Mechanics (Vector Treatment)
- Kinematics: position, velocity, acceleration vectors; relative motion
- Newton's laws: free body diagrams; constraint forces; non-inertial frames (fictitious forces)
- Work-energy theorem; potential energy functions; conservation of energy
- Linear momentum: impulse-momentum theorem; collision types; rocket propulsion
- Angular momentum: $\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{p}$; conservation; gyroscopes
- **Rigid body rotation**: moment of inertia tensor; parallel axis theorem; rolling without slipping
- **Statics**: torque equilibrium; center of gravity

### Oscillations and Waves
- Simple harmonic motion: $\ddot{x} + \omega_0^2 x = 0$; phase, amplitude, frequency
- Damped oscillations: under, over, critically damped; driven oscillations; resonance
- Wave equation: transverse and longitudinal waves; energy transport
- Superposition: beats; standing waves in strings and pipes; Fourier synthesis
- Doppler effect (sound); shock waves; Mach number

### Thermodynamics (Rigorous)
- Ideal gas: kinetic theory; equipartition theorem; degrees of freedom
- First law: internal energy, heat, work for various processes (isothermal, adiabatic, isochoric)
- Entropy: Clausius inequality; $dS = \delta Q_{rev}/T$; entropy of mixing; statistical interpretation
- Second law: Kelvin-Planck and Clausius statements; Carnot theorem
- Heat pumps; refrigerators; coefficient of performance

## PHYS1114: General Physics II

### Electromagnetism
- Electrostatics: Gauss's law in integral and differential form ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \rho/\varepsilon_0$)
- Electric potential: Poisson's and Laplace's equations; multipole expansion
- Conductors; capacitors; dielectrics: polarization, displacement field
- Magnetostatics: Biot-Savart; Ampere's law; magnetic vector potential
- Electromagnetic induction: Faraday's law; self and mutual inductance; RL circuits
- **Maxwell's equations**: displacement current; electromagnetic wave equation; Poynting vector
- EM waves: polarization; Fresnel equations at interfaces

### Optics
- Geometric optics: ray tracing; thin lens equation; mirror equation
- Interference: Young's double slit; thin film; Michelson interferometer
- Diffraction: single slit; diffraction grating; Rayleigh criterion for resolution
- Polarization: Malus's law; birefringence

### Modern Physics
- **Special relativity**: postulates; time dilation $\Delta t = \gamma \Delta t_0$; length contraction; relativistic momentum and energy $E^2 = (pc)^2 + (m_0c^2)^2$
- **Quantum mechanics (intro)**: photoelectric effect; de Broglie wavelength; Heisenberg uncertainty principle
- Atomic models: Bohr model; hydrogen energy levels $E_n = -13.6/n^2$ eV
- Nuclear physics: radioactive decay; binding energy; fission and fusion

## PHYS1312: Honors Physics

Covers the same material as PHYS1112+1114 but at greater mathematical depth:
- Emphasis on vector calculus formulation (divergence, curl, Stokes' theorem)
- More formal treatment of Lagrangian mechanics; Hamilton's principle of least action
- Condensed matter physics intro: crystal structures; band theory; semiconductors

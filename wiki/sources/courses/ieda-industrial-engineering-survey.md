## 概述

IEDA 2010「現代物流與工業工程」是香港科技大學 IEDA 學系的第二年必修概覽課，適合已具備中學數學基礎的本科生修讀。課程以 12 場講座橫跨工業工程核心子領域，包括最佳化（線性規劃）、博弈論（納什均衡）、生產力與流程分析、排隊理論（M/M/1 模型、Little's Law）、庫存管理（EOQ、報童模型）、收益管理（容量配置）、供應鏈管理（牛鞭效應）及專案管理（CPM/PERT）。課程不設指定教材，教授提供自編講義，旨在向學生展示 IE 各主題並非孤立知識，而是管理資源以優化不確定環境下決策的統一框架。修讀本課後，學生可進一步選修 IEDA 2200（工程管理）、IEDA 3010（運籌學 I）及 IEDA 3250（隨機模型）等專業課程。

## Course Overview

**IEDA 2010: Modern Logistics and Industrial Engineering** is a required survey course for IEDA undergraduates at [[wiki/entities/hkust|HKUST]]. It provides a broad introduction to the discipline before students branch into specialized courses. The course draws on lecture notes spanning optimization, stochastic modeling, operations management, and supply chain topics.

Lecture topics (Fall 2016 offering) covered across 12 sessions:

| Session | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to IE |
| 2 | Optimization |
| 3 | Game Theory |
| 4 | Probability Basics |
| 5a/5b | Productivity and Process Analysis / Process Selection |
| 6a/6b | Midterm Review |
| 7a/7b | Waiting Line Analysis (Queueing) |
| 8a/8b/8c | Inventory Management |
| 9 | Revenue Management |

## Key Topics Covered

### Optimization
Introduction to formulating and solving linear programs. Emphasis on setting up decision variables, objective functions, and constraints. Connects to [[linear-programming]] as the core methodology.

### Game Theory
Strategic interaction between rational agents; Nash equilibrium concept; applications in competitive pricing and supply chain coordination. See [[game-theory]].

### Queueing Theory
Waiting line models (M/M/1 and variants), Little's Law, throughput analysis. See [[wiki/concepts/queueing-theory|Queueing Theory]] and [[wiki/concepts/littles-law|Little's Law]].

### Inventory Management
EOQ model, newsvendor problem, safety stock, reorder points. Bridges into the [[wiki/concepts/newsvendor-problem|Newsvendor Problem]] and [[wiki/concepts/supply-chain-management|Supply Chain Management]].

### Revenue Management
Capacity allocation, overbooking, dynamic pricing. See [[wiki/concepts/revenue-management|Revenue Management]].

### Supply Chain Management
End-to-end coordination of suppliers, manufacturers, and distributors; bullwhip effect; coordination mechanisms. See [[wiki/concepts/supply-chain-management|Supply Chain Management]].

### Project Management
CPM/PERT, network diagrams, critical path identification, project scheduling under uncertainty.

## Course Significance

IEDA 2010 functions as the intellectual map of the entire IEDA program. Its value is in showing how topics that appear disparate — optimization, probability, queueing, inventory — are all facets of the same underlying problem: managing resources under uncertainty to optimize outcomes. Students who understand the connections between these topics are better positioned to apply advanced methods in later courses.

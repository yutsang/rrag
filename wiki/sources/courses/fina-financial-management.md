## 概述

FINA2303 財務管理為非金融商學院專業（會計、管理、市場營銷方向）的二年級學生提供企業財務管理的實用知識，以 Ross、Westerfield 及 Jordan《公司財務基礎》（Fundamentals of Corporate Finance）為標準教材，先修要求為 ACCT2010 會計學原理。相較於金融專業的 FINA2203，本課程更側重管理應用視角而非嚴格的數學推導，使非金融背景的管理者能夠理解財務語言並作出知情的商業決策。課程涵蓋財務報表分析（杜邦分解、比率分析）、資本預算的多種評估方法（NPV、IRR、回收期、獲利指數）、融資工具的選擇（銀行貸款、債券、可轉換債券、IPO）、百分比銷售額預測法（pro forma 報表）及短期財務管理（現金轉換週期、貿易信用成本）。學生完成課程後能夠閱讀上市公司年報、評估資本投資項目的可行性、估算外部融資需求（EFN），以及理解槓桿對企業風險與回報的影響。本課程為商學院學生提供跨職能的財務視角，是管理、會計及供應鏈領域高年級課程不可或缺的財務基礎。

## Course Overview

FINA2303 is the corporate finance course for non-FINA BBA students (e.g., accounting, management, marketing tracks). It covers the same conceptual terrain as FINA2203 but with heavier emphasis on managerial interpretation over mathematical derivation.

**Textbook**: Ross, Westerfield, Jordan, *Fundamentals of Corporate Finance* (alternate textbook track)

## Key Topics

### Financial Statement Analysis
- Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement — see [[financial-statements]]
- Ratio analysis: liquidity, solvency, profitability, efficiency — see [[financial-ratios]]
- DuPont decomposition: ROE = Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
- Common-size statements; trend analysis; benchmarking

### Capital Budgeting (Applied)
- NPV, IRR, Payback, Discounted Payback, Profitability Index
- Mutually exclusive vs. independent projects; capital rationing
- Incremental cash flow analysis: identifying relevant vs. irrelevant cash flows
- Post-audit of capital projects

### Financing Decisions
- Debt instruments: bank loans, bonds, leasing, revolving credit
- Equity: IPO vs. secondary offerings; private vs. public
- Hybrid: convertible bonds, preferred shares, mezzanine finance
- Credit analysis: coverage ratios, leverage ratios; lender perspectives

### Long-Term Financial Planning
- Percentage-of-sales forecasting; pro forma statements
- EFN (external financing needed): when growth requires new funding
- Sustainable growth rate: $g^* = \frac{ROE \times b}{1 - ROE \times b}$ where $b$ = retention ratio

### Short-Term Finance
- Operating cycle and cash conversion cycle
- Short-term borrowing: trade credit (cost of skipping discount), bank line of credit
- Cash management and float

### Risk Management Overview
- Business risk vs. financial risk
- Hedging with derivatives: FX forwards, interest rate swaps
- Insurance strategies

## 概述

ECON2103 經濟學概論為工程、理科及非經濟商學院學生提供微觀與宏觀經濟學的入門訓練，以 Mankiw《經濟學原理》（Principles of Economics，Cengage 標準教材）為主要教材，無特定先修要求，是二年級學生最廣泛修讀的社科通識課程之一。微觀部分系統建立供需框架（市場均衡、彈性分析、消費者與生產者剩餘、無謂損失）、市場結構理論（完全競爭、獨佔、寡佔的 Cournot/Bertrand 模型入門）及外部性與公共財的政策分析；宏觀部分介紹 GDP 核算、通脹測量、失業類型、短期波動的 AD-AS 模型及長期增長的 Solow 模型框架。課程特別結合香港情境（聯繫匯率制度對貨幣政策的限制、香港作為外向型開放小經濟體）幫助學生理解政策選擇。學生完成課程後能夠分析價格管制、稅收、貿易政策的福利效應，理解利率政策與財政乘數的宏觀作用機制，以及評估通脹、失業與增長之間的短期-長期權衡。這些概念是理解商業決策環境、市場設計及公共政策分析的共同語言，對 IEDA 運籌學及工業工程學生尤具有補充視角的價值。

## Course Overview

**Textbook**: N. Gregory Mankiw, *Principles of Economics*

Survey course covering both micro and macro in one semester. Target audience: engineering, science, and non-economics business students who need economic literacy.

## Microeconomics Component

### Supply and Demand
- Demand curve: law of demand; shifts (income, prices of related goods, expectations)
- Supply curve: law of supply; shifts (input costs, technology, number of sellers)
- Market equilibrium; surplus and shortage; price adjustment
- Price elasticity: $\varepsilon_D = \frac{\% \Delta Q}{\% \Delta P}$; elastic vs. inelastic; revenue implications — see [[microeconomics]]

### Consumer and Producer Surplus
- Consumer surplus: area below demand curve above price
- Producer surplus: area above supply curve below price
- Total surplus = social welfare; efficiency of market equilibrium
- Deadweight loss from taxes, price ceilings, price floors

### Market Structures
- Perfect competition: price-taking; long-run zero profit
- Monopoly: MR = MC; markup pricing; natural monopoly regulation
- Oligopoly: Cournot, Bertrand models (intro) — see [[game-theory]]
- Monopolistic competition: product differentiation; zero long-run profit with excess capacity

### Externalities and Public Goods
- Negative externalities (pollution): social cost > private cost; Pigouvian tax
- Positive externalities (education): subsidy solution
- Public goods: non-rival, non-excludable; free-rider problem

## Macroeconomics Component

### National Income Accounting
- GDP: expenditure approach (C + I + G + NX)
- Real vs. nominal GDP; GDP deflator; CPI; inflation
- Unemployment: frictional, structural, cyclical; natural rate

### Long-Run Growth
- Solow growth model (intro): capital accumulation, steady state
- Role of savings rate, technology, human capital
- Convergence and divergence across countries

### Short-Run Fluctuations
- Aggregate demand (AD) and aggregate supply (AS) model
- Demand-side shocks vs. supply-side shocks; stagflation
- Keynesian multiplier: $\Delta Y = \frac{1}{1-MPC} \Delta G$
- See [[macroeconomics]]

### Monetary and Fiscal Policy
- Money supply, money demand; money market equilibrium
- Monetary policy: central bank tools (interest rates, open market operations)
- Fiscal policy: government spending, taxation; crowding out
- HK-specific context: currency board limits monetary policy independence

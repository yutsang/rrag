## 概述

IEDA 4960「畢業設計」（Final Year Project）是香港科技大學 IEDA 學系本科生課程的頂點科目，橫跨整個學年，由學系教授擔任指導老師，要求學生在四年 IEDA 課程知識基礎上獨立完成一個完整的工業工程、決策分析或財務工程研究項目。典型專案方向涵蓋三大類別：運籌學與最佳化（供應鏈網絡設計、排程與資源分配、隨機優化與模擬、整數規劃或動態規劃的新型應用）、數據分析與機器學習（運營/財務/物流的預測建模、需求預測與收益管理、NLP 商業應用）及財務工程與風險（衍生品定價模型、投資組合優化、VaR/CVaR 風險度量）。學生須完成專案計劃書（早期學期，3–5 頁，涵蓋目標、方法論與時間表）、進度報告（學期中）、期末報告（學術論文格式：引言、文獻綜述、方法、結果、結論）及海報展示（學系 FYP 海報展，按標準海報模板呈現動機、問題建模、方法、結果與結論）。評核涵蓋報告質量（方法嚴謹性、表達清晰度）、貢獻的意義與創新性、海報展示質量及導師對研究態度與進度的評估，是學生進入業界或攻讀 OR/IE 博士課程前最重要的獨立研究訓練機會。

## Course Overview

**IEDA 4960 Final Year Project (FYP)** is the capstone experience for IEDA undergraduate students. Students undertake an independent project under faculty supervision, integrating knowledge and methods from across the IEDA curriculum. The FYP spans an academic year and concludes with a poster presentation and written report.

Available materials: `IEDA_FYP_Poster.png` (poster template), `Guideline for Project Plan and Progress Report.pdf`.

## Typical Project Types

IEDA FYP projects generally fall into three categories:

**Operations Research and Optimization**:
- Supply chain optimization (network design, inventory, routing)
- Scheduling and resource allocation
- Stochastic optimization and simulation
- Applications of integer programming, dynamic programming, or heuristics

**Data Analytics and Machine Learning**:
- Predictive modeling for operations/finance/logistics
- Demand forecasting and revenue management
- Natural language processing for business applications
- Real-world dataset analysis with Python/sklearn

**Financial Engineering and Risk**:
- Derivatives pricing models
- Portfolio optimization
- Risk measurement (VaR, CVaR)
- Algorithmic trading strategy development

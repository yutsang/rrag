## 概述

ACCT3610 商業法律與倫理為會計學系三年級學生介紹香港普通法體系下的商業法律框架，以本地法律教材配合香港法院判例為主要參考資料，先修要求為 ACCT2010 及 ACCT2200，是香港會計師公會（HKICPA）專業資格考試（QP）的相關前期預備課程。課程涵蓋四大法律領域：合同法（要約與承諾的成立要件、合同撤銷事由如欺詐性失實陳述與脅迫、違約救濟的期望損失與實際損失賠償）、侵權法（過失侵權的 Donoghue v. Stevenson 注意義務三步驟、職業過失、雇主替代責任）、香港公司法（《公司條例》下的薩羅門法人格分隔原則、董事的受信義務與謹慎義務、公司清盤程序）及代理法與僱傭法（明示授權與表見授權、《僱傭條例》的最低通知期與法定假期要求）。商業倫理模塊介紹功利主義與義務論的倫理理論框架、Carroll 的 CSR 金字塔及 HKICPA 職業道德守則（誠信、客觀性、保密性五大基本原則）。學生完成課程後能夠識別商業交易中的常見法律風險、理解審計獨立性的法律與倫理要求，以及就企業治理問題進行有依據的道德推理，是會計及商業法律實踐的重要基礎。

## Course Overview

Covers Hong Kong business law under the common law system, with 4 topic areas plus a business ethics module. Structured for accounting students but relevant to all business majors.

## Key Topics

### Topic 1: Legal System and Contract Law
- Hong Kong legal system: common law heritage; role of precedent (stare decisis); legislation hierarchy
- **Contract formation**: offer and acceptance; consideration; intention to create legal relations; capacity
- Terms: express vs. implied; conditions vs. warranties; exclusion clauses (UCTA)
- Vitiating factors: misrepresentation (fraudulent, negligent, innocent); mistake; duress; undue influence
- **Remedies for breach**: damages (expectation, reliance, restitution); specific performance; injunction

### Topic 2: Tort Law
- **Negligence**: duty of care (Donoghue v. Stevenson); breach; causation; remoteness; damages
- Occupiers' liability; professional negligence (accountants, lawyers)
- **Product liability**: manufacturer liability; defective products
- Vicarious liability: employer responsible for employee torts in course of employment
- Economic loss: Hedley Byrne principle for negligent misstatement

### Topic 3: Company Law (Hong Kong Companies Ordinance)
- Incorporation: separate legal personality (Salomon principle); limited liability
- Company types: private limited, public limited, unlimited; incorporation procedure
- **Corporate governance**: directors' duties (fiduciary, care and skill); conflicts of interest
- Share capital: types (ordinary, preference); dividends; rights issues; buybacks
- Insolvency: winding up (voluntary, compulsory); liquidator powers; wrongful trading

### Topic 4: Agency and Employment
- **Agency law**: creation of agency; actual vs. apparent authority; undisclosed principals
- Agent's duties: obedience, care, accounting, personal performance
- **Employment law**: contract of employment; wrongful dismissal vs. unfair dismissal
- Employment Ordinance (HK): statutory minimum notice; statutory holidays; MPF obligations

### Business Ethics
- Ethical theories: utilitarianism, deontology (Kant), virtue ethics
- **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)**: Carroll's pyramid; stakeholder theory (Freeman)
- Accounting ethics: HKICPA Code of Ethics; fundamental principles (integrity, objectivity, confidentiality)
- Whistleblowing; earnings management; audit independence

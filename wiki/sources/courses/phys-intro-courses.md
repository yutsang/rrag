## 概述

PHYS1001、PHYS1002 及 PHYS1003 構成香港科技大學面向非物理專業學生的基礎物理入門序列，無特定先修要求（高中物理背景有助於 PHYS1001/1002），採用 Serway & Jewett《物理學》（非理工版）或 Giancoli《物理學》作為標準教材。PHYS1001 系統涵蓋古典力學（牛頓定律、功能定理、動量守恆）與熱力學（理想氣體定律、熱力學第一與第二定律、Carnot 效率）；PHYS1002 介紹電磁學（Coulomb 定律、Gauss 定律、Faraday 感應定律、Maxwell 方程組的定性描述）與波動光學（干涉、折射、Snell 定律）；PHYS1003 以能源與環境為主題，從熱力學效率上限出發，分析太陽能、風能、核能等可再生能源技術的物理原理及氣候科學（溫室效應、碳預算）。學生完成課程後能夠應用牛頓定律分析力學系統、計算熱機效率與能量轉換損耗、理解基本電路（歐姆定律、RC 電路時間常數）及電磁感應原理，並對全球能源系統轉型的物理約束具備定量認識。這些基礎知識對工業工程師（機械系統受力分析）、環境工程師（能源系統建模）及 IoT 應用開發者（感測器電路基礎）均具有實際的工程支撐價值。

## PHYS1001: Mechanics and Thermodynamics

### Classical Mechanics
- **Kinematics**: 1D and 2D motion; displacement, velocity, acceleration; projectile motion
- **Newton's Laws**: F = ma; free body diagrams; normal force, friction, tension
- **Work and Energy**: work-energy theorem $W = \Delta KE$; conservative forces; potential energy; energy conservation
- **Momentum**: impulse; conservation of momentum; elastic and inelastic collisions; center of mass
- **Rotational motion**: angular velocity, torque, moment of inertia; rotational kinetic energy; angular momentum conservation
- **Gravitation**: Kepler's laws; orbital mechanics; escape velocity

### Thermodynamics
- Temperature and heat; thermal expansion; heat capacity; calorimetry
- Ideal gas law: $PV = nRT$; kinetic molecular theory; rms speed
- Laws of thermodynamics: zeroth (thermal equilibrium); first ($\Delta U = Q - W$); second (entropy, Carnot efficiency)
- Heat engines and refrigerators; Carnot cycle; efficiency $\eta = 1 - T_L/T_H$

## PHYS1002: Electricity, Magnetism, and Waves

### Electrostatics
- Coulomb's law; electric field $\mathbf{E}$; superposition; field lines; Gauss's law
- Electric potential; equipotential surfaces; capacitance; dielectrics
- Energy stored in electric field: $U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2$

### Electric Circuits
- Ohm's law; resistivity; power dissipation $P = I^2R$
- Series and parallel resistors; Kirchhoff's laws; RC circuits; time constant

### Magnetism
- Magnetic force on moving charge: $\mathbf{F} = q\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}$; Lorentz force
- Biot-Savart law; Ampere's law; solenoids
- Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction; Lenz's law; transformers
- Maxwell's equations (qualitative); electromagnetic waves and spectrum

### Waves
- Wave properties: wavelength, frequency, period, wave speed; transverse vs. longitudinal
- Interference: constructive/destructive; standing waves; resonance
- Sound: decibel scale; Doppler effect
- Light: reflection; refraction; Snell's law; total internal reflection; lenses; optics

## PHYS1003: Energy and Environment

- Global energy consumption: fossil fuels, renewables, nuclear
- Thermodynamic limits on conversion efficiency; entropy and waste heat
- Solar energy: photovoltaic effect; solar thermal; capacity factors
- Wind, hydro, geothermal: resource potential and limitations
- Nuclear energy: fission, fusion; radioactive decay; half-life
- Climate science: greenhouse effect; global warming potential; carbon budget

## Relevance to Engineering

- Mechanics underpins structural analysis, fluid mechanics, and manufacturing process forces
- Thermodynamics connects to energy systems engineering and environmental impact assessment
- Electricity/circuits knowledge is foundational for sensor systems and IoT in industrial settings

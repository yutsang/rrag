## 概述

ISOM3310 電子商務與網絡分析為工商管理三年級學生教授如何運用數據分析工具優化電子商務業績，由 Jack Teh 博士主持，綜合使用 Laudon & Traver《電子商務》（第十四版）、Gupta & Davin《數字營銷》（Harvard Business Publishing）及 Osterwalder《商業模式生成》（Business Model Canvas）三本教材，先修要求為 ISOM2400 電子商務及 ISOM2500 商業統計。課程以重項目評估為特色（網站設計、商業分析報告、演示），涵蓋商業模式畫布（九大構建模塊）的數字企業映射、客戶獲取漏斗（認知→考慮→購買→忠誠）的轉化率優化、搜索引擎優化（頁面 SEO、外部鏈接建設、技術 SEO）、Google Analytics 的流量分析與同期群留存分析、A/B 測試的統計顯著性設計，以及點擊流 Markov 模型與協同過濾推薦系統的網絡數據挖掘方法。學生完成課程後能夠設計並評估數字行銷活動的投資回報（CAC 對比 CLV）、以 Google Analytics 儀表板監控業務健康指標、運用 A/B 測試驗證網頁優化假設，並理解內容行銷、WeChat 官方帳號及跨境電商的亞洲市場特殊性，直接支撐電商運營、數字行銷分析師及增長駭客等職業方向。

## Course Overview

**Instructor**: Dr. Jack Teh (jteh@ust.hk), Summer 2021
**Textbooks**:
- Laudon & Traver, *E-Commerce: Business, Technology, Society* (14th ed.)
- Sunil Gupta & Joseph Davin, *Digital Marketing* (Harvard Business Publishing, 2015)
- Alexander Osterwalder, *Business Model Canvas*

Applied e-business analytics course. Assessment is project-heavy: website design, business analytics reports, presentations.

## Key Topics

### Business Model Canvas
- 9 building blocks: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partners, Cost Structure
- Mapping digital businesses onto the Canvas: Netflix, Google, Airbnb examples
- Unicorn analysis: why many well-funded startups fail (unit economics)
- See [[ecommerce-models]]

### Digital Marketing
- **Customer acquisition funnel**: awareness → consideration → intent → purchase → loyalty
- **Content marketing**: creating value before selling; inbound vs. outbound
- **Email marketing**: segmentation; lifecycle campaigns; A/B subject line testing — see [[ab-testing]]
- **Social media marketing**: organic reach decline; paid social; influencer marketing
- **WeChat marketing**: official accounts; mini-programs; WeCom (enterprise)
- Attribution modeling: last-click, first-click, linear, time-decay

### Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- How search engines work: crawling, indexing, ranking
- On-page SEO: title tags, meta descriptions, header hierarchy, keyword density
- Off-page SEO: PageRank; link building; domain authority
- Technical SEO: site speed, mobile-first indexing, structured data (Schema.org)
- Local SEO: Google My Business; NAP consistency

### Web Analytics
- Google Analytics: sessions, users, pageviews, bounce rate, session duration
- Traffic sources: organic, direct, referral, paid, social
- Conversion rate optimization (CRO): funnel visualization; exit rates
- **Cohort analysis**: retention by acquisition cohort
- **A/B testing** for web: statistical significance; minimum detectable effect — see [[ab-testing]]

### Web Data Mining
- Log file analysis: extracting visitor patterns from server logs
- Clickstream analysis: Markov models for navigation paths
- Content-based vs. collaborative filtering for product recommendations — see [[recommendation-systems]]
- Link analysis: HITS algorithm; PageRank computation

### Security and Payment
- SSL/TLS; HTTPS importance for SEO and trust — see [[cryptography]]
- Electronic payment systems: gateway, processor, acquirer relationships
- Cryptocurrency e-payments (intro) — see [[defi]]
- Fraud prevention: velocity checks, device fingerprinting, ML-based scoring

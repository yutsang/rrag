## 概述

ISOM1700 工商管理概論為大學一年級學生提供現代企業各職能領域的全景介紹，無特定先修要求，採用冬季學期短課格式，以香港本地企業（國泰航空、無國界貿易等）的真實個案為教學素材。課程系統涵蓋企業戰略分析工具（PESTLE、SWOT、Porter 五力模型、平衡計分卡）、運營管理基礎（流程圖、瓶頸識別、前置期分析）、供應鏈管理（庫存的 EOQ 邏輯、最後一公里物流取捨）、需求預測方法（移動平均、指數平滑）及可持續發展框架（三重底線、CSR 報告）。杜邦戰略利潤模型將財務報表指標與運營績效指標串聯，培養學生以「數字語言」評估業務決策的能力。學生完成課程後能夠應用 Porter 五力模型分析行業競爭格局、繪製供應鏈流程圖並識別改善機會、計算簡單的庫存與產能指標，以及撰寫結構化的商業個案分析報告。本課程為商學院各專業方向奠定跨職能思維基礎，對工程及理科學生了解商業運作邏輯尤為重要，亦是 ISOM2700 運營管理的直接概念先導。

## Course Overview

Introductory survey course covering the major functional areas of a business firm. Uses real-world Hong Kong companies (Cathay Pacific, Trade Without Borders) as case studies. Winter semester format.

## Key Topics

### Business Strategy
- **PESTLE analysis**: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental scanning
- **SWOT analysis**: strengths/weaknesses (internal) vs. opportunities/threats (external)
- **Porter's Five Forces**: competitive intensity analysis — see [[porters-five-forces]]
- Balanced Scorecard: financial, customer, internal process, learning & growth perspectives — see [[balanced-scorecard]]
- DuPont Strategic Profit Model: connecting operations metrics to ROE

### Sustainability and CSR
- Triple bottom line: people, planet, profit
- CSR reporting; sustainability-driven innovation (IDEO cases)
- Supply chain labor standards: J&J global employment guidelines
- Social enterprise models

### Operations and Supply Chain
- **Process management**: process flow diagrams; throughput time; cycle time; bottleneck
- **Quality management**: TQM principles; process capability; defect reduction
- **Capacity planning**: utilization; expansion timing decisions
- **Inventory management**: EOQ logic; trade-off between holding and ordering costs — see [[newsvendor-problem]]
- **Demand management and forecasting**: moving averages, exponential smoothing; forecast error
- **Distribution**: logistics network design; last-mile tradeoffs — see [[supply-chain-management]]

### Product and Innovation
- Product life cycle: introduction, growth, maturity, decline
- Shanzhai (山寨): Chinese manufacturing ecosystem; innovation through imitation
- Stage-gate process for new product development

### Global Trade
- Globalization drivers; trade barriers and free trade agreements
- Global sourcing decisions: TCO (total cost of ownership); risks
- HK as trade hub: re-export role, financial services nexus

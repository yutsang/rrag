## 概述

MGMT1110 管理學概論為工商管理學院一年級學生系統介紹現代管理理論的四大職能（計劃、組織、領導與控制），以 Robbins、Coulter 等《管理學》（Management，Pearson 全球版）為基礎教材，無特定先修要求，是工商管理各專業方向（會計、金融、市場、運營、信息系統）的共同入門必修課程。課程橫跨九大主題，涵蓋管理演進歷史（泰勒科學管理、Fayol 行政管理、Hawthorne 行為學派）、PESTLE 與 Porter 五力的環境分析工具、Schein 的組織文化層次模型、Simon 有限理性與決策偏差（可得性捷思、錨定效應）、MBO/SMART 目標設定與 BCG 矩陣策略規劃、Mintzberg 的結構配置（機械官僚與有機結構）、Maslow/Herzberg/Vroom 期望理論的激勵框架、俄亥俄州立大學的領導行為研究與情境領導理論，以及以平衡計分卡為核心的控制系統設計。學生完成課程後能夠應用 SWOT 分析評估組織策略選擇、運用激勵理論設計工作豐富化方案、識別組織結構的集權/分權取捨，以及理解管理決策中的認知偏差來源，為後續 MGMT2110 組織行為學及各職能方向課程建立必要的管理學理論語境。

## Course Overview

**Structure**: 9 topics (syllabus: Fall 2018)
Topics cover the classic management functions plus contemporary themes of organizational culture, external environment analysis, and motivation/leadership theories.

## Key Topics

### Topic 1: Introduction to Management
- Four management functions: Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling
- Management levels: top, middle, first-line; managerial roles (Mintzberg: interpersonal, informational, decisional)
- Management skills: technical, human, conceptual; changing requirements across levels
- History of management thought: scientific management (Taylor), administrative (Fayol), behavioral, quantitative, contingency

### Topic 2: External Environment Analysis
- **PESTLE**: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental
- **Porter's Five Forces**: competitive rivalry, buyer power, supplier power, new entrants, substitutes — see [[porters-five-forces]]
- **SWOT analysis**: internal (strengths/weaknesses) and external (opportunities/threats)
- Environmental uncertainty: complexity and dynamism; organizational responses

### Topic 3: Organizational Culture
- Levels of culture: artifacts, espoused values, basic assumptions (Schein)
- Strong vs. weak cultures; culture and performance
- Organizational socialization; managing cultural change

### Topic 4: Decision Making
- Rational decision-making model: 8 steps; bounded rationality; satisficing (Simon)
- Decision types: programmed vs. non-programmed; structured vs. unstructured
- Biases: availability heuristic, representativeness, anchoring, escalation of commitment — see [[behavioral-economics]]
- Group decision-making: brainstorming; Delphi method; nominal group technique

### Topic 5: Planning
- Types of plans: strategic, tactical, operational; single-use vs. standing plans
- MBO (Management by Objectives): SMART goals
- Strategic planning process: mission, environmental analysis, strategy formulation, implementation
- Tools: BCG matrix; Ansoff matrix (market penetration, development, product development, diversification)

### Topic 6: Organizing
- Organizational structures: functional, divisional, matrix, team-based, network
- Span of control; chain of command; centralization vs. decentralization
- **Mechanistic vs. organic** structures: stability vs. flexibility
- Mintzberg's structural configurations: simple structure, machine bureaucracy, professional bureaucracy, adhocracy

### Topic 7: Motivation
- **Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs**: physiological → safety → social → esteem → self-actualization
- **Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory**: hygiene factors vs. motivators
- **Expectancy Theory (Vroom)**: Motivation = Expectancy × Instrumentality × Valence
- **Equity Theory (Adams)**: compare input/outcome ratios to referents; adjust behavior
- Job design: job enrichment, job characteristics model (Hackman & Oldham)
- See [[organizational-behavior]]

### Topic 8: Leadership
- Trait theories: early leadership research
- **Behavioral theories**: Ohio State (initiating structure vs. consideration); Michigan Studies
- **Situational leadership**: Hersey & Blanchard; Fiedler's contingency model
- **Transformational vs. Transactional** leadership: vision, inspiration, intellectual stimulation
- Servant leadership; authentic leadership; ethical leadership

### Topic 9: Controlling
- Control process: set standards → measure → compare → take corrective action
- Types: feedforward, concurrent, feedback control
- Balanced Scorecard as control system — see [[balanced-scorecard]]
- Financial controls: budgets, ratio analysis — see [[financial-ratios]]

## 概述

ACCT2010 會計學原理 I 為工商管理學院二年級學生奠定財務會計的系統基礎，以 Horngren、Harrison 及 Oliver《會計學》或 Weygandt《會計學原理》等國際主流教材為參考，遵循國際財務報告準則（IFRS）框架，無特定先修要求，是商學院所有學生的必修核心課程。課程以複式記帳的借貸邏輯為起點，完整介紹八步驟會計循環（從交易識別到期末結帳分錄），深入講解三大財務報表的編制原理：損益表（營業收入 − 銷貨成本 − 營業費用）、資產負債表（資產 = 負債 + 股東權益）及現金流量表（直接法與間接法）。關鍵會計政策包括應收賬款的呆賬撥備法（年齡分析法）、存貨計價方法（FIFO、加權平均成本法、LCNRV 下限測試）及固定資產折舊（直線法、雙倍遞減法）的稅務影響。學生完成課程後能夠從原始憑證出發編制完整財務報表、計算基本財務比率（流動比率、毛利率、資產報酬率）並與行業基準比較，以及識別常見的財務報表呈現方式差異，為 ACCT2200 管理會計、FINA2203 企業財務及所有需要財務報表分析的高年級課程提供不可或缺的會計語言基礎。

## Key Topics

### Accounting Equation and Double-Entry
- Fundamental equation: Assets = Liabilities + Equity
- Debits and credits: T-accounts; journal entries; posting to ledger
- Normal balances: assets and expenses (debit); liabilities, equity, revenues (credit)
- Trial balance: detecting arithmetic errors; adjusted trial balance

### The Accounting Cycle
1. Identify transactions → 2. Journal entries → 3. Post to ledger → 4. Trial balance → 5. Adjusting entries → 6. Adjusted trial balance → 7. Financial statements → 8. Closing entries

- **Adjusting entries**: accruals (revenues/expenses earned/incurred but not recorded); deferrals (prepaid expenses, unearned revenues)
- Depreciation as an adjusting entry: Straight-line, units of production, double-declining balance — see [[depreciation]]
- Closing entries: transfer revenue/expense balances to retained earnings

### Financial Statements
- **Income Statement**: Revenue − COGS − Operating Expenses = Net Income
- **Balance Sheet**: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity (snapshot view)
- **Statement of Cash Flows**: Operating, Investing, Financing activities (direct and indirect methods)
- **Statement of Changes in Equity**: retained earnings reconciliation
- See [[financial-statements]]

### Current Assets
- Cash and cash equivalents; bank reconciliation
- **Accounts receivable**: allowance for doubtful accounts; percentage of sales vs. aging method
- **Inventories**: periodic vs. perpetual systems; FIFO, LIFO, weighted average cost; LCNRV

### Non-Current Assets
- Property, plant & equipment: cost model vs. revaluation; depreciation methods
- Intangible assets: amortization; goodwill (business combinations)
- Impairment testing: recoverable amount

### Liabilities and Equity
- Current liabilities: AP, accrued liabilities, deferred revenue, current portion of long-term debt
- Long-term liabilities: bonds payable; effective interest method for amortization of premium/discount
- Shareholders' equity: share capital, additional paid-in capital, retained earnings, treasury shares

### Basic Ratio Analysis
- Liquidity: current ratio, quick ratio
- Profitability: gross margin, net margin, ROA, ROE
- See [[financial-ratios]]

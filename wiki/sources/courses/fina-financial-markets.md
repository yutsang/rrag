## 概述

FINA1303 金融市場概論為一年級工商管理學生提供現代金融體系結構與功能的全景介紹，無特定先修要求，以 Mishkin《貨幣、銀行與金融市場》或類似標準金融市場教材為參考。課程涵蓋金融體系的資金流通機制（儲蓄者→金融中介機構→借款者）、貨幣市場工具（國庫券、商業票據、HIBOR）、債券市場定價（票息率、到期殖利率、存續期間）、股票市場估值（股利折現模型、市盈率）、衍生品市場基礎（期貨、期權的損益圖）及外匯市場（港幣聯繫匯率制度、利率平價）。課程特別以香港作為國際金融中心的制度背景為例，介紹 HKMA、SFC 及 HKEX 的監管框架、H 股與滬深港通的市場連通機制。學生完成課程後能夠閱讀金融市場新聞、理解利率變動對債券價格的影響、識別基本的期權策略，並以 CAPM 框架評估股票的系統性風險。本課程是 FINA2203 企業財務及 FINA3103 投資學的概念先導，亦為工程及理科學生補充必要的金融市場素養。

## Key Topics

### Financial System Overview
- Role of financial markets: channeling funds from savers to borrowers
- Primary vs. secondary markets; organized exchanges vs. OTC
- Financial intermediaries: banks, insurance companies, investment funds, pension funds
- Regulatory framework in Hong Kong: HKMA, SFC, HKEX

### Money Markets
- Instruments: T-bills, commercial paper, certificates of deposit, repos
- HIBOR (Hong Kong Interbank Offered Rate); Fed Funds rate
- Yield calculations; discount vs. add-on yield

### Bond Markets
- Government bonds; corporate bonds; HK Exchange Fund bills
- Bond pricing fundamentals: coupon, yield-to-maturity, price-yield relationship
- Duration as price sensitivity measure — see [[fixed-income]]
- Credit ratings: Moody's, S&P, Fitch; investment grade vs. high yield

### Equity Markets
- Stock valuation: DDM (dividend discount model), P/E multiples — see [[financial-ratios]]
- Market efficiency hypothesis: weak, semi-strong, strong forms
- HKEX structure; H-shares, Red Chips; Stock Connect with mainland China
- IPO process; rights issues; share buybacks

### Derivatives Markets
- Futures and forwards: hedging interest rate and currency risk
- Options: calls and puts; payoff diagrams; basic strategies (covered call, protective put) — see [[options-pricing]]
- Warrants and CBBCs popular in HK retail market
- Hang Seng Index futures; Hang Seng Tech Index

### Foreign Exchange
- Spot and forward FX rates; bid-ask spread
- HKD Linked Exchange Rate System (LERS); currency board mechanics
- Interest rate parity; covered vs. uncovered

### Portfolio Management Basics
- Return and risk measures; correlation and diversification
- Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM): $E[R_i] = R_f + \beta_i(E[R_m] - R_f)$ — see [[portfolio-theory]]

## 概述

ISOM2400 互聯網與電子商務概論為工商管理二年級學生提供電子商務生態系統的全面入門，以 Laudon & Traver《電子商務：商業、技術與社會》（E-Commerce: Business, Technology, Society）為標準教材，先修要求為 ISOM2010 電腦與商業應用，是 ISOM3310 電商與網路分析的直接先修課程。課程覆蓋電商核心商業模式（B2C 虛擬商戶、B2B 市場平台、C2C 及 B2G）的收入流分析、Web 技術基礎（TCP/IP、HTTP/HTTPS、DNS、HTML/CSS 概覽）、數字營銷渠道（SEO/SEM、電郵行銷、社交媒體廣告、內容行銷漏斗與 A/B 測試）及電子支付基礎設施（支付閘道、PCI-DSS 合規、Alipay/WeChat Pay 等數字錢包）。課程特別關注亞洲電商生態（阿里巴巴、京東、Shopee 的平台模式差異）及香港個人資料私隱法例（PDPO）框架。學生完成課程後能夠評估電商商業模式的可持續性、設計基本的數字行銷漏斗、識別網絡安全威脅（SSL/TLS、詐騙檢測），並理解平台網絡效應對市場結構的影響，為後續數字商業策略與網路分析課程奠定實踐基礎。

## Course Overview

**Textbook**: Laudon & Traver, *E-Commerce: Business, Technology, Society*

Introductory e-commerce course covering business models, underlying web technologies, digital marketing channels, and security/payment infrastructure.

## Key Topics

### E-Commerce Business Models
- B2C, B2B, C2C, B2G model types; revenue streams (subscriptions, advertising, transaction fees)
- E-tailer models: virtual merchant, brick-and-click, catalog merchants
- Marketplace models: Amazon, Alibaba; network effects and platform dynamics
- Freemium models; razor-and-blades pricing
- See [[ecommerce-models]]

### Web Technology Fundamentals
- Internet infrastructure: TCP/IP, HTTP/HTTPS, DNS; client-server model
- HTML/CSS basics; web server architecture — see [[web-architecture]]
- Cloud hosting: CDNs; scalability considerations
- Mobile commerce: responsive design; native apps vs. mobile web

### Digital Marketing
- **SEO**: organic search; on-page (keywords, meta tags) vs. off-page (backlinks) factors; Google PageRank
- **SEM/PPC**: Google Ads; cost-per-click; Quality Score; ad auction mechanics
- **Email marketing**: open rate, CTR, list segmentation; CAN-SPAM compliance
- **Social media marketing**: Facebook Ads, Instagram, WeChat; viral coefficient
- **Content marketing**: inbound funnel; conversion optimization (CRO); A/B testing — see [[ab-testing]]
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC) vs. customer lifetime value (CLV) — see [[crm]]

### Online Payments and Security
- Payment gateway; merchant account; acquiring vs. issuing banks
- Credit card processing flow; PCI-DSS compliance
- Digital wallets: PayPal, Alipay, WeChat Pay, Apple Pay
- Cryptocurrency payments (intro) — see [[defi]]
- SSL/TLS encryption; digital certificates; HTTPS — see [[cryptography]]
- Fraud detection: address verification, 3D Secure

### Legal and Ethical Issues
- Intellectual property online: copyright, DMCA, fair use
- Privacy: cookies, GDPR principles; Hong Kong PDPO
- Consumer protection in e-commerce; returns and chargebacks
